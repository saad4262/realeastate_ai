# AU compliance check

## Rules

1. **AI listing copy = draft only.** Human must approve before publish (ACL misleading conduct risk).
2. Alerts/email: consent + **working unsubscribe** (Spam Act 2003).
3. Price guides / estimates: NSW underquoting (Property & Stock Agents Act) applies to AI-generated guides too. Prefer confidence bands; never invent numbers in the LLM.
4. Every AI claim that depends on data needs a **citation** (listing id / data point).
5. **Tenant screening must not auto-reject.** AI may summarise; human decides.
6. Only use media the agency has licensed — never scrape/hotlink portal images (Hardingham).
7. Privacy Act / APPs: minimise collection; Sydney residency preferred; deletion flow when accounts exist.
8. Show AI disclosure in UI where generated content appears (AU voluntary AI Safety Standard).
