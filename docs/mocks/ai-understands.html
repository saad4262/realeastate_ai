Phase 4.5 · AI understands, then decides · for your review

What is decided, and six details to settle

Your decisions are in; these smaller ones shape the ADR. Each has a suggestion — say yes, or change it. Screens 1–5 show them in place.

Decided (2026-10-31)

Decided
  • A + B + C now: understand every new enquiry, save what it found only through an approval card, conditions on what it understood. Mid-run decisions later (4.5b).
  • Runs on every new enquiry while the skill “AI understands enquiries” is on (off until switched on).

1 · Which leads

Enquiries, inspection requests and appraisal requests — from the website and recorded by hand. Never private offers (they are owners’ and admins’ only). Suggested.

2 · What it reads

The enquiry’s message and the first email in its conversation, if any — as 4.2 does. Not notes, not other records. Later emails do not re-run it (that is 4.5b).

3 · What it says

Type: buyer, seller, landlord, tenant, investor or general — or “not sure”. Urgency: high or normal, with one sentence why. A one-line summary. For a buyer: price from / to, bedrooms, bathrooms, suburbs, property type, timeline. For a seller: the address of their own home and when.

4 · Numbers are found, never made

Every figure must be found by code in the enquiry’s own words (“$750k”, “3 bed”); a figure it cannot find is left out and the panel says so. “Around 800” is kept only as written words in the summary, never as a price. Suggested — this is non-negotiable 4.

5 · Who sees it

Whoever may see the lead sees what the AI understood. Saving the brief needs the same permission as editing the contact; the card goes to the lead’s agent (or the workflow’s owner).

6 · Cost

One short call per enquiry, under the agency’s daily AI budget (the same pool as “AI decides”, default $2 a day). Out of budget: the lead simply shows no chips that day, and a workflow condition on it does not match.

The AI reads each new enquiry and says what kind of person it is, how urgent, a one-line summary, and the brief in their own words. Shown on Leads; usable in workflows; saved to a contact only when someone approves. One short call per enquiry, within the agency’s daily AI budget.
