Forward the calls you miss

On your mobile, open the phone keypad, type this and press call:

**61*0480999888**20#Your phone shows "Call forwarding activated".
voicemail
This replaces your voicemail. A caller you miss hears a short message from Pakenham Realty and gets a text instead. To turn it off later, dial ##61#. Your carrier may charge a forwarded call like a normal call. Most unlimited plans include it.
