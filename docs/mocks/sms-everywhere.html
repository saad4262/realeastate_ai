Phase 3.4 · SMS everywhere · for the client's approval

Five rules to approve

Texts cost the agency money and fall under Australia's Spam Act, so these rules decide who gets a text, from which number, when, and how many. Please approve each one or say what to change. The screens 1–6 in the bar above show them in place.

1 · The agency number

To approve

A text goes from the person's own agent's Twilio number. When that agent has no number yet, or nobody is assigned, it goes from the agency number: one of the agency's existing numbers that the owner picks in Phone & SMS (the owner's own by default). If there is no agency number either, that person is skipped and the review says "No number to send from".

Alternative: buy one extra number only for the agency, held by an admin account. Costs one more number a month. See screen 6.

2 · Who may get a marketing text

To approve

The same rule as Bulk email: the person agreed to marketing, or enquired with the agency in the last 24 months, or is a client (seller, landlord, tenant). On top of that, for texts: an Australian mobile only, and never anyone who replied STOP to any of the agency's numbers. Every text ends with "Reply STOP to opt out", which cannot be removed. Consent and STOP are checked at Prepare and again at the moment each text goes.

Alternative: texts only to people who gave recorded consent (stricter, fewer people reached). A one-to-one reply in a conversation is not marketing and is not affected.

3 · Sending hours: 9 am to 8 pm

To approve

Bulk texts, texts to matched buyers and workflow texts go only between 9 am and 8 pm on the agency's time zone, the same hours the AI call back uses (the owner can change them in Missed calls → Settings). Anything approved outside those hours waits for the next morning. The instant missed-call text is not held back: the caller has just rung.

4 · At most one automatic text per caller per 24 hours

To approve

If both "Missed call text" and a missed-call workflow with "Send SMS" are on, the caller gets only the first of them in any 24 hours, never two. Bulk texts and texts to matched buyers are a person's deliberate choice and are not capped this way; they still respect STOP, consent and the daily limit.

5 · 100 texts per number per day

To approve

Each agent's number sends at most 100 texts a day, counting every kind together: missed-call texts, bulk, matched buyers and workflows. Past the limit, a text is skipped with "agent's daily limit reached" and nothing is sent. Bulk batches stay at 20 people each, as in Bulk email.

The number 100 is a setting and can be raised or lowered.
