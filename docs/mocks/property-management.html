Phase 7 · Property Management (#15) · for your review

Rentals: six decisions

Today the platform advertises rentals and tags landlords and tenants, then stops at “Leased”. This adds what a property manager does every week afterwards — the tenancy, routine inspections, repairs, the lease ending — on the same contacts, calendar, approval cards and workflows. The money does not move here.

1 · People and property here; money stays in their PM software

Suggested — strongly

Rent collection, the trust account, bond lodgement, landlord statements and paying tradespeople stay in the agency’s property management software (PropertyMe, Console Cloud, PropertyTree…). Trust accounting is regulated and audited every year in each state; getting a cent wrong is a licensing matter. The platform records the rent and the bond as facts typed in by a person, to show them — it never holds or moves money.

Alternative: build trust accounting. Months of work, an auditor’s sign-off, state by state — and agencies that manage rentals already pay for software that does it.

2 · A tenancy belongs to the property, not the listing

Suggested

A new record, the tenancy: the property, its landlord(s), its tenant(s), the lease start and end, the rent per week, the bond (amount and reference), and the property manager. Marking a rent listing “Leased” offers a card that starts one from the listing’s rent and the applicant you chose; existing tenancies come in by hand or a CSV import. The listing stays the advertisement; the property outlives both (non-negotiable 1).

Alternative: wait for a PropertyMe / Console Cloud connector and read tenancies from there. That is a connector, and connectors are version 2.

3 · Routine inspections: the agency enters its state’s rules, the platform checks them

Suggested

In Settings the agency sets the notice it gives (days), how far apart inspections must be (months) and how long after the lease starts the first may be. Booking an inspection checks the date against those numbers and shows each check; the entry notice to the tenant is an email on an approval card. After the visit the manager adds notes and photos on the phone; the AI drafts a short report for the landlord — a draft, sent only by approval (non-negotiable 7).

We do not write the law into the code: the rules differ by state and change. The numbers in the mock are samples the agency would confirm.

4 · Repairs: tenants report by a private link; spending is a person’s decision

Suggested

Each tenancy has a private “Report a repair” link (in the welcome email and on request) — no account, no password. The report arrives like an enquiry and the AI reads it as in 4.5: what is wrong, and whether it looks urgent (no hot water, a gas smell, a burst pipe…). An urgent one alerts the manager at once. Telling the landlord and asking a tradesperson to quote is an approval card. Agreeing a price or approving a quote is human only — the AI never agrees to spend the landlord’s money.

5 · Lease ending: a nudge, real figures, no rent suggested

Suggested

A new workflow trigger, “N days before a lease ends” (the agency picks N; 90 suggested, to leave room for the notice periods). It makes a task and a card: an email asking the landlord whether to offer a renewal, with recent leases nearby from our own leased listings (SQL). The AI writes the words only; the new rent, if any, is a blank the manager fills. Rent-increase and notice-to-vacate notices use the state’s official forms — not in this step.

6 · Who, and how it is switched on

Suggested

Each tenancy has a property manager — any member, like the agent on a listing (a tenancy_manager link, not a new role). Managers see their tenancies; owners and admins see all; everything goes through can(). The Rentals area appears when the agency switches on the skill “Property management” — off until switched on (ADR 0069), so sales-only agencies never see it.

Not in this step

Later
  • Money: rent, arrears, the trust account, bond lodgement, statements, paying invoices — in their PM software.
  • A connector to PropertyMe / Console Cloud / PropertyTree (version 2, with the CRM connectors).
  • Rental applications and tenant screening; logins for tenants or landlords.
  • The state’s official forms (rent increase, notice to vacate, condition report).
