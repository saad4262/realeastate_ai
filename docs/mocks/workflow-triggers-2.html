Phase 4.4 · More workflow triggers · for the client's approval

Twelve new triggers, five decisions

Fourteen things can start a workflow today. These twelve more come from the brief and use only what the platform already records, so they need no new account or provider. Screen 1 shows them all; screen 2 shows automations an agency might build with them.

1 · Build all twelve in this round

To approve

Contacts: gets a role, brief added or changed, goes quiet, unsubscribes. Leads: assigned, not contacted in time. Listings: price changes, an open home finishes. Email: a customer writes in. Calendar: an appointment is cancelled. Deals: an offer comes in, settlement is near. Or pick the ones you want first.

2 · What counts as "contacted" for a new lead

To approve

A lead counts as contacted once any of these happens: an email or text is sent to them from the platform, a call is logged, an appointment is booked, or someone marks the lead "contacted". The agency picks the time: 1 to 72 hours.

3 · "Goes quiet" means nothing for N days

To approve

No email in or out, no text, no call, no note, no appointment, no enquiry for N days (30 to 365; 90 suggested). Archived contacts and anyone unsubscribed never count. Each contact starts a workflow once per quiet spell.

4 · Someone unsubscribes: inside steps only

To approve

A workflow on "unsubscribes" may only do things inside the agency: a note, a task, a reminder, tell a colleague. It can never email or text that person — the platform refuses such a step for this trigger.

5 · "Price changes" means the numbers

To approve

The trigger fires when the listing's price numbers change (price from / to, or rent per week), up, down or either — not when only the wording changes ("Offers over" to "Expressions of interest"). A drop by at least a set percentage can be a condition.

Not in this round — each needs something first

Later
  • An email bounces — needs bounce events from the email provider (Resend webhooks).
  • A no-show — needs a "didn't come" outcome on appointments.
  • Someone attended an open home — needs attendance recorded at open homes.
  • The AI spots a likely seller — part of Phase 4.5 ("AI understands, then decides").
  • Marketing triggers (campaign published, cost per lead) — version 2, with the Marketing Studio.
