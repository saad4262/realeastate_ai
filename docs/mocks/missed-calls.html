Missed calls

How missed calls are handled for every agent. Whether anything runs at all is decided in Skills: nothing is texted or called until a skill is switched on there.

Switched on in Skills

Each runs for an agent only when the agency and that agent have switched it on.

What the caller hears

Played when a missed call is forwarded to us. It replaces the agent's voicemail.

Read out in an Australian English voice.

The text

Only while "Missed call text" is on in Skills. Sent from the agent's Twilio number within a minute. An approved message, never written by the AI.

Fields: {{first_name}} (or "there" for a new caller) {{agent_first_name}} {{agency_name}}
smartphone

How the AI calls back

Only while "AI calls back" is on in Skills. It calls from the agent's own verified number.

  • It says it's an AI assistant for the agent, and that the call may be recorded.
  • It asks what the call is about, what they're looking for and the best time to call, then offers a call back from the agent.
  • It never gives prices, valuations or promises, and never books anything by itself.
  • It only calls people who just called you, never a list.

Calling hours

The text goes straight away at any hour. AI calls wait for these hours. A 10 pm missed call is called back at 8 am.

Limits

So a fault or a flood of calls can't run up your Twilio bill.

lockOne text and one AI call per caller in 24 hours
lockNobody who replied STOP
