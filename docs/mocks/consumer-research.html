Research — seven decisions

Three skills that help a buyer understand: a suburb (Suburb AI), several homes side by side (Comparison AI) and one home (Property Analyst). The hard part is the data: our own records are a small slice of the market. Each decision has a recommendation; say "all recommended" or name the ones to change.

  1. 1. Never a value, a prediction or a verdict.No estimated value, no "fair price", no "will grow", no "good investment", no "overpriced" — not from us and not from the AI. The listing pages already say "this platform does not estimate values". Recommended: yes, everywhere in C5, and the guide is told so.
  2. 2. Medians only with enough sales — and said to be ours.A median sold price or median days on the market for a suburb is shown only when at least 10 sales on this site in the last 12 months back it; otherwise "Not enough sales on this site yet" with the sales we do have listed. Every figure is labelled "from listings on this site — not the whole market". Recommended: 10 sales / 12 months. Alternative: a lower bar (5) — more suburbs get a figure, each one less reliable.
  3. 3. Free public data: ABS Census 2021, housing tables only.Per suburb, from the ABS QuickStats (Census 2021, the latest until the 2026 results are released): population, dwellings by type, owned / mortgaged / rented shares, median weekly rent, median monthly mortgage repayment, average household size. Loaded once from the ABS file, dated, linked. Not ancestry, religion, income or anything about kinds of people. Recommended: yes — housing figures only, labelled "ABS Census 2021". Alternative: no census data in v1 (suburb pages show only our own records).
  4. 4. Comparison: 2–4 homes, every figure from SQL, the AI only explains.From the shortlist or a "Compare" button on listings. The table: price, beds/baths/cars, land, days on the market, price changes, last sale at the address, stamp duty estimate (C1, at your buyer type), and the state's checklist differences (C2). The AI writes trade-offs that cite columns and rows; a line with a figure not in the table is dropped (the C2 check). Recommended: yes.
  5. 5. Property Analyst: "Explain this home", cited, no value.On a listing and on an address not for sale: the home's facts, its history (C2 records), and comparable homes from SQL — same suburb, ±1 bedroom, sold or listed in the last 12 months — shown as a list, never averaged into a value. The AI writes 3–5 cited points. Recommended: yes; comparables as a plain list.
  6. 6. Suburb pages are public and cached./suburb/vic/pakenham: our live listings and recent sales, the medians when allowed, the census figures, a map; "Compare with another suburb". The AI paragraph is on a button and cached for a day. The guide gets suburb_profile, compare_homes and explain_home. Recommended: yes. All three skills answer only, so all are ON by default (ADR 0080).
  7. 7. Cost guard.Each AI paragraph (suburb, comparison, explanation) is cached — a suburb for a day, a home until its listing changes — and rate-limited like Due-Diligence. Recommended: yes.
