Property Description

Verified by Selling Principal

Contemporary Elegance & Low-Maintenance Lifestyle in Prime Pakenham Pocket

Positioned in an exceptionally quiet and boutique enclave of Rogers Street, this architecturally curated two-storey townhouse redefines modern executive living. Perfectly suited for discerning first-home buyers, downsizers desiring low-maintenance sophistication, or high-yield portfolio investors seeking strong capital retention in Melbourne's south-eastern corridor.

Stepping through the wide entry foyer, you are greeted by warm timber floorboards and high ceilings that lead into a luminous open-plan family living and dining sanctuary. At the culinary heart of the home, the gourmet kitchen commands attention with 40mm engineered stone benchtops with breakfast bar, 900mm stainless steel freestanding gas cooktop and oven, integrated dishwasher, and an expansive walk-in pantry.

Accommodation is thoughtfully zoned across both levels. The lavish master suite boasts a private sun-drenched sanctuary accompanied by an oversized walk-in robe and an opulent ensuite featuring full-height porcelain tiling, a frameless walk-in glass shower, and a floating dual vanity. Two additional bedrooms upstairs are generously scaled with tailored built-in robes, serviced by a central designer bathroom and separate powder room.

Seamless indoor-outdoor synergy is achieved via glass sliding doors extending onto an alfresco entertainer's paved courtyard, fully fenced for privacy and framed by low-maintenance perimeter plantings—ideal for relaxed weekend barbecues and pet owners.
