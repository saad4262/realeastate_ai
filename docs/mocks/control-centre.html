Phase 4.6 + 4.7 · Control Centre · for the client's approval

Four decisions to approve

The Control Centre shows everything the platform did by itself, and lets the agency decide how much it may do without asking. These choices shape it. The screens 1–4 in the bar above show them in place.

1 · Who sees what

To approve

Owners and admins see everything the agency's automations did. An agent sees only what ran for them: their own workflows, their own skills, calls to their number, and anything waiting for their approval. Nobody sees another agency's.

2 · Each kind of step has a floor

To approve

Every kind of step has the lowest level it may run at, set by the platform: a note or a task can be automatic; an email the AI wrote always needs approval; a contract, money or deleting records is for people only and the AI can only suggest it. An owner may raise a step's level for the agency (make an automatic one ask first) but never lower it below its floor. See screen 4.

Alternative: fixed levels, nobody changes them. Simpler, less control for the agency.

3 · How long the record is kept

To approve

The full trail of every event (trigger, what the AI understood and decided, every step, who approved, the result) is kept for 12 months, then deleted. Today, runs are kept 90 days.

4 · Telling people about failures

To approve

Anything that failed (a send the provider refused, a broken workflow) puts a bell notification to the workflow's owner at once. Owners also get one email at 8 am listing yesterday's failures, only when there were any.

Permission levels

How much each kind of step may do without asking. The platform sets the lowest level for each; you may make any step ask first, for the whole agency. Agents can't change these.

Automatic

Runs by itself and is recorded here. Tasks, notes, tags, reminders, an approved template.

Needs approval

Prepared, then waits for a person. Anything the AI wrote for a customer, big changes to records.

People only

The AI may suggest it; it can never do it. Money, contracts, legal steps, permissions, deleting records.
