Its runs

verified_userThe AI only chooses one of your options — it never writes the steps, sends anything, or makes up an option. It reads the enquiry’s message and the newest email from the client (for “an appointment is completed”, its outcome note) — nothing else. When it is not sure, “Not sure” runs. Its choice and why are in the run’s log. Under Skills → AI decisions in workflows; while that is off, every run takes “Not sure”.
