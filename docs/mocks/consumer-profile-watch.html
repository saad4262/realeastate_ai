Profile and watch — seven decisions

Four skills that remember things for one buyer: homes they like (shortlist), addresses they follow (Property Monitor), what they are looking for (Personal Property AI), and better saved-search emails (Smart Alerts). Each decision has a recommendation; say "all recommended" or name the ones to change.

  1. 1. The shortlist is a basic feature, not an AI skill.A heart on every listing and search card; a Shortlist page with a private note on each home. Signed-in only. No switch: nothing acts by itself. Recommended: yes. (The table already exists; nothing uses it yet.)
  2. 2. Property Monitor watches an ADDRESS, not an ad."Watch this address" on a listing and on an address not for sale. It tells the buyer when the address is listed for sale or rent (Not for Sale → For Sale), when the price changes (recorded since 3 Nov 2026), when it goes under offer and when it sells. All from our own records, every figure from SQL. Recommended: yes — up to 50 addresses per buyer. It acts by itself (emails), so it is OFF until the buyer switches it on (ADR 0080).
  3. 3. "No longer advertised" — say it or not?When a watched listing is withdrawn, our public history leaves withdrawn campaigns out (ADR 0013). Recommended: say only "This address is no longer advertised on this site", never "withdrawn" and never why — the buyer could see the ad disappear anyway. Alternative: say nothing; the event list just stops.
  4. 4. How the buyer hears. Recommended: one email a day at 8 am in their time zone, only when something happened, with every change in it and an unsubscribe link; and a Watching page that always shows the full history. No SMS, no push. Alternative: an email the moment something changes (more emails, more cost).
  5. 5. Personal Property AI: what it learns, and from what.From the buyer's OWN actions only: their saved searches, shortlist, watched addresses and the filters the guide used in their chats. Stored as plain, editable facts — suburbs, price range, bedrooms, property types — counted by code. Never their income, family, age or anything about them as a person; never free text guessed by a model. Recommended: yes; opt-in (OFF until switched on), every fact shown with where it came from, editable, and one "Forget everything" button. When on, the guide starts from it ("You usually look in Pakenham, 3+ bedrooms — still the case?").
  6. 6. Smart Alerts upgrade.A saved-search email adds two short sections, both from SQL: price changes on homes it already sent you, and what sold in that suburb since the last email. Recommended: yes; the existing summary paragraph rules stay (no figures in the model's words, ADR 0080 #7).
  7. 7. Agencies never see any of it.Who shortlisted, watched or profiled what stays the buyer's, as saved searches and chats already are (ADR 0011). The platform admin console keeps its count of shortlisted homes per user — nothing more. Recommended: yes — no "12 people are watching this" for agencies in this version.
