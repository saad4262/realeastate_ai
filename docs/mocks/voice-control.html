Phase 7.1 · Voice control (#23) · for your review

Talk to the assistant: five decisions

Everything the brief names already works by typing. Voice adds a microphone to the same assistant — the same tools, the same rules, the same approval cards. Nothing new can be done by voice that cannot be done by typing.

1 · Who turns speech into words

Suggested: the browser

The browser’s own speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari; on phones too): free, no new account or key, works today. The browser maker (Google, Apple, Microsoft) processes the audio; we never receive or keep a recording, only the words. Firefox has none — the mic is hidden there and typing works as now.

Alternative: a speech service on our server (e.g. OpenAI Whisper or Deepgram) — every browser, better with names and Australian accents, but a new provider, a key and a cost per minute, and the audio passes through us. Can be added later if the browser’s is not good enough.

2 · After speaking: check, then send

Suggested

What you said appears in the box; you read it, fix a name if needed, and press Send (or say nothing and tap Send). Speech recognition mishears names — “John Citizen” becomes “Jon Citizen” — and the assistant would then look up the wrong person.

Alternative: send by itself after a pause (faster, hands-free) — a person can switch it on in Settings.

3 · Answers read aloud: off until switched on

Suggested

A speaker button on every answer reads it aloud (the browser’s own voice, free, Australian English where the device has it). “Read answers aloud” in Settings does it for every answer — useful in the car. Off by default: an office full of talking laptops is no one’s idea of help.

4 · Changes still need a tap

Suggested — strongly

“Remind me to call John tomorrow” makes the same card as typing it, and the card needs a TAP on Approve. Saying “approve” or “yes” does nothing. A misheard word must never send an email, move a deal or change a record.

5 · Where

Suggested

Everywhere the assistant is: LocalAgent AI and the side panel, for agents and owners, on desktop and phone. It listens for English (Australia), and each person can change that. It is a skill, “Voice for the assistant”, and like every skill (ADR 0069) it stays off until the agency switches it on.

Alternative: on for everyone from day one. Voice changes nothing by itself, but the rule so far has no exceptions, and one exception invites the next.

Not in this step

Later
  • A wake word (“Hey LocalAgent”) — always listening needs a native app and raises privacy questions.
  • Talking to the AI on a phone call — that is the Retell call back (Phase 3.3), not the console.
  • Dictating a whole email into the composer — easy to add once this is in.
