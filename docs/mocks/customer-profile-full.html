Decisions in this mock

  1. Emails, SMS, Calls and Messages are four separate tabs. None of them lists another channel. Only Timeline shows everything together, as history.
  2. Messages is built empty. No channel feeds it yet, so it shows an empty state and no invented data.
  3. The Enquiries tab becomes Leads, with a summary on top: totals by status, kind and source, first and latest lead, average first reply and open deals. All from SQL.
  4. Agency activity = its own listings. Views and saves on its listings, and searches in suburbs where it has a listing, because a search belongs to a place and not to one listing.
  5. An agent gets the same, for the listings they are named on.
  6. Admin sees everything: the agency’s part on the customer page, and the whole site on the website account.
  7. New page: Listing activity. Who looked at our listings, including signed-in people who are not customers yet, by name and email. Anonymous visitors stay a count.
  8. A customer is tied to a website account by an enquiry from that account, or by the same email. The email tie is labelled “not confirmed”, because sign-up does not verify an email.
  9. Answered calls are logged by hand. They ring the agent’s mobile and never reach the platform. Missed calls and AI call backs fill in by themselves once Twilio is connected.
  10. Hot, warm and quiet come from fixed rules with the reason shown, not from a model. “What to do next” is the same.
  11. The AI summary runs only when pressed, carries a label, is given no figures, and is rejected if it writes one.
  12. Admin gets a People list and the full customer profile, read only.
Before go-live: the privacy policy today says activity is kept to improve the service, not that agencies see it by name. Testing can go ahead as you decided; this has to change before real customers use the site.
