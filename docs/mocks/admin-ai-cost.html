Decisions for you

Each one has my recommendation. Say “sab recommended” or name the number you want changed.

1. Where it goes

Recommended: a new “AI cost” item in the admin sidebar, at /ai-cost, with one page per agency under it.

No new tables and no migration. It reads ai_run with the indexes already there.

2. Who can see it

Recommended: platform admins only.

Agencies do not see costs, because they are not billed for AI today. An agency view can come when plans have an AI allowance.

3. Currency

Recommended: US dollars only.

The price list and the $20 limit are in USD. Showing AUD would need an exchange rate we do not keep.

4. Showing people by name

Recommended: agents and owners by name inside an agency; buyers never.

The public site stays one line, “Public site (buyers)”. Admins already see agents’ work, but nobody needs to see what one buyer cost.

5. Calls with no agency on them

Recommended: fix it from now on, and show old rows as “Not tied to an agency”.

Found while checking: Morning Brief, Important emails and floorplan reading are saved without an agency, even though they belong to one. Without this fix those costs would be missing from the agency’s line. The fix is small and needs no migration. Old rows stay as they are.

6. How the $20 limit is described

Recommended: say on the page exactly what it stops and what it does not.

Found while checking: the limit stops the assistant, AI skills, alert summaries and buyers’ tasks. The public property chat and the buyer tools (due diligence, renovation, research) do not check it. They have their own daily caps on requests, so their spend counts towards the $20 but can carry on past it. The hard ceiling is the spend limit in the Anthropic console, so please check that one is set. Making the public tools obey the $20 limit would be a separate change, and I have not included it.

7. Warnings

Recommended: on the page only for now.

An email at 80% of the limit would be a small later addition, on the existing notice email. You asked for the screen, so it is not in this build.

8. Changing a limit from this page

Recommended: no. The page is read only.

Limits stay in Vercel’s settings, where a change is deliberate. The page shows what they are set to.
